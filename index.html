How It Works

Step 1: Lead Initiation

Prospects initiate interaction through web chat, landing page forms, or marketing campaigns.

Step 2: AI Conversation

The AI agent engages the user, asks qualifying questions, and gathers key details like company size, budget, and intent.

Step 3: Lead Qualification

The system evaluates responses and assigns a qualification score based on predefined criteria.

Step 4: CRM Update

Lead records are created or updated in Salesforce with all captured details and conversation history.

Step 5: SDR Handoff

Qualified leads are routed to human SDRs for follow-up, ensuring faster deal closure.
